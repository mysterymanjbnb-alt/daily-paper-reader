## 摘要
融合差分隐私的认证防御已被证明在卷积神经网络（CNN）上有效，能够针对范数有界对手提供严格的鲁棒性保证。然而，在自注意力架构如今主导深度学习格局的背景下，像素差分隐私（PixelDP）的认证鲁棒性行为仍很大程度上未被探索。鉴于Transformer已对从数字世界到物理世界的日常应用产生深远影响，通过差分隐私式的稳定性来研究认证鲁棒性至关重要。为填补这一研究空白，我们重新审视视觉Transformer中的这一构造，并识别出一种在卷积设定中很大程度上被隐藏的失效模式。当在图像块嵌入（patch embedding）之后注入噪声时，继承分组 $\ell_1$ 敏感度界的拉普拉斯机制在不同噪声尺度下都会坍缩至随机猜测水平的准确率，而高斯机制则仍可训练。这一对比分离出了失败的根源：不是注入的噪声本身，而是敏感度约束的几何性质。我们表明，由继承的 Δ_{1,1} 投影所诱导的衰减会随层宽度和核大小增加，其遵循随机矩阵尺度 $C/(\sqrt{M}+\sqrt{N})$。将 $\ell_1$ 型约束替换为谱范数约束，可在不同数据集和架构上消除这种坍缩，但会产生一个根本性障碍：修复后的模型不再满足标准拉普拉斯证书所需的敏感度条件。我们通过隐私损失的集中性，推导出在 $\ell_2$ 敏感度下拉普拉斯机制的与维度无关的 $(ε, δ)$-隐私保证，从而解决了这一不匹配问题。

## Abstract
Certified defenses that incorporate differential privacy have proven effective on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), furnishing rigorous robustness guarantees against norm-bounded adversaries. However, the certified robustness behavior of Pixel Differential Privacy (PixelDP) remains largely unexplored with the self-attention architecture now dominating the deep-learning landscape. Given that the Transformer has a profound impact on our daily applications from the digital world to the physical world, it is crucial to study certified robustness through differential-privacy-style stability. To fill this research gap, we revisit this construction in Vision Transformers and identify a failure mode that is largely hidden in the convolutional setting. When noise is injected after the patch embedding, the Laplace mechanism with the inherited grouped $\ell_1$ sensitivity bound collapses to chance-level accuracy across noise scales, whereas the Gaussian mechanism remains trainable. This contrast isolates the source of failure: not the injected noise itself, but the geometry of the sensitivity constraint. We show that the attenuation induced by the inherited $Δ_{1,1}$ projection increases with layer width and kernel size according to a random-matrix scale $C/(\sqrt{M}+\sqrt{N})$. Replacing the $\ell_1$-type constraint with a spectral-norm constraint eliminates the collapse across datasets and architectures, but creates a fundamental obstacle: the repaired models no longer satisfy the sensitivity condition required by the standard Laplace certificate. We resolve this mismatch by deriving a dimension-free $(\varepsilon,\ δ)$-privacy guarantee for the Laplace mechanism under $\ell_2$ sensitivity through concentration of the privacy loss.
