## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作模型与世界-动作模型日益流行，但仍受限于物理交互数据稀缺、机构间孤岛化以及任务异构等瓶颈。一种自然的联邦解决方案是让每个客户端通过参数高效微调来适配共享基础模型，从而避免交换全模型更新。然而，联邦化这些适配器并非易事：朴素聚合可能使不兼容的更新相互纠缠，而将 MoE 风格路由引入联邦聚合则可能稀释专业化并破坏专家选择的稳定性。我们提出 RoboFL，它将 MoSAIC（Mixture of Slotted Adapters，槽式适配器混合）实例化用于联邦世界-动作学习。MoSAIC 将本地训练的 LoRA 适配器直接安装为服务器端 MoE 的专家分支。服务器端路由器在这些先验信息引导的分支上学习 token 分配，同时联合优化路由与专家参数。前瞻到动作路由蒸馏（Foresight-to-Action Routing Distillation, FARD）对齐模型三条路径上的路由，而路径共识专家聚合（Path-Consensus Expert Aggregation, PCEA）将完整的专家更新转换为紧凑的全局适配器，以便进行个性化再分发。在 RoboTwin 2.0、RLBench 以及真实世界 Franka 机械臂上的实验表明，采用结构化专家组装的 RoboFL 具有优越性：在 Franka 机械臂上，它比集中式 PEFT InternVLA-A1 高出 12.23%，同时相对于基于 MoE 的联邦 VLA 基线，将每轮客户端通信量最多减少 86.81%。

## Abstract
Vision-language-action and world-action models are increasingly popular, yet remain bottlenecked by physical interaction data that is scarce, institutionally siloed, and task-heterogeneous. A natural federated solution is to let each client adapt a shared foundation model through parameter-efficient fine-tuning, avoiding the exchange of full-model updates. However, federating these adapters is nontrivial, as naive aggregation can entangle incompatible updates, while incorporating MoE-style routing into federated aggregation may dilute specialization and destabilize expert selection. We present RoboFL, which instantiates MoSAIC (Mixture of Slotted Adapters) for federated world-action learning. MoSAIC directly installs locally trained LoRA adapters as the expert branches of a server MoE. Server-side routers learn token assignments over these prior-informed branches while jointly refining routing and expert parameters. Foresight-to-Action Routing Distillation (FARD) aligns routing across the model's three paths, while Path-Consensus Expert Aggregation (PCEA) converts complete expert updates into a compact global adapter for personalized redistribution. Experiments on RoboTwin 2.0, RLBench, and a real-world Franka robot arm show the superiority of RoboFL with structured expert assembly, as it outperforms centralized PEFT InternVLA-A1 by 12.23% on the Franka arm, while reducing per-round client communication by up to 86.81% relative to MoE-based federated VLA baselines.
