## 摘要
协作具身人工智能（CEAI）使多个物理智能体能够在动态环境中协同感知、推理和行动。有效的通信对CEAI至关重要，然而CEAI智能体不仅需要交换大规模多模态观测数据，还需要在长时程内交换与任务相关的见解、意图和交互信息。本文研究Token通信（TokCom）作为CEAI的原生智能接口，其中Token既共同作为通信的紧凑语义载体，又作为生成式基础模型（GFM）的基本推理单元。我们首先讨论TokCom如何支持具身智能体之间的见解共享、意图对齐和交互控制。然后，我们提出一种由任务自适应通信协议驱动的TokCom辅助CEAI框架。该协议由紧凑码本、语法规则和上下文示例组成，引导基于GFM的收发器将消息提炼为紧凑Token，并在无线传输后重建它们。一个协作物体运输案例研究表明，所提出的TokCom框架在保持任务效率的同时显著降低源载荷比特消耗，并在噪声信道下表现出鲁棒性。最后，我们概述未来研究方向。

## Abstract
Collaborative embodied artificial intelligence (CEAI) enables multiple physical agents to perceive, reason, and act cooperatively in dynamic environments. Effective communication is essential for CEAI, yet CEAI agents must exchange not only large multimodal observations but also task-relevant insights, intents, and interactive information over long horizons. This article investigates token communication (TokCom) as a native intelligence interface for CEAI, in which tokens serve jointly as compact semantic carriers for communication and fundamental inference units for generative foundation models (GFMs). We first discuss how TokCom supports insight sharing, intent alignment, and interactive control among embodied agents. We then propose a TokCom-assisted CEAI framework driven by a task-adaptive communication protocol. Comprising a compact codebook, syntax rules, and contextual examples, this protocol guides GFM-based transceivers to distill messages into compact tokens and reconstruct them after wireless transmission. A case study on collaborative object transport demonstrates that the proposed TokCom framework substantially reduces the source payload bit consumption while preserving task efficiency and showing robustness under noisy channels. Finally, we outline future research directions.
